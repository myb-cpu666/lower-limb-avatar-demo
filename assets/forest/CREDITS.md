# Forest art

Selected models and the shared gradient texture from **KayKit: Forest Nature
Pack 1.0 FREE**, created by Kay Lousberg. Downloaded from the author's free tier
on 2026-10-08. The original license is included as `LICENSE.txt`.

Source: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-forest

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0).

The original glTF models, buffers and texture are retained. Scene placement,
uniform scale, lighting, terrain, guardian and game props are specific to this
prototype. No paid-tier assets are included.
